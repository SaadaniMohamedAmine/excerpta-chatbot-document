3. Financial Overview

The third quarter demonstrated significant resilience in core operational metrics despite broader macroeconomic headwinds. Revenue from the primary enterprise segment grew by 14% year-over-year, driven largely by increased adoption of our advanced analytics modules.

Operating margins improved to 22.4%, reflecting ongoing cost-optimization efforts initiated in Q1. Particularly notable was the reduction in customer acquisition cost (CAC) by 8%, suggesting that recent shifts in marketing strategy are yielding more efficient conversion funnels.

However, forward-looking guidance must account for potential supply chain disruptions anticipated in early Q4. While inventory levels are currently sufficient to meet projected demand for the next 45 days, subsequent fulfillment may experience delays if current geopolitical tensions escalate.
