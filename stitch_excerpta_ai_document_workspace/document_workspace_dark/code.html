Q3 Financial Overview

The third quarter demonstrated significant resilience across all major operating segments. Total revenue increased by 14% year-over-year, driven primarily by strong performance in the cloud infrastructure division.

Operating expenses were carefully managed, resulting in a 220 basis point improvement in operating margin. Notably, R&D expenditure remained stable at 18% of total revenue, reflecting our ongoing commitment to long-term innovation without compromising near-term profitability.

Cash flow from operations reached a record $4.2 billion for the quarter, allowing for continued share repurchases and strategic investments.
